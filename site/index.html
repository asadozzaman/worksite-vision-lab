03 / THE RESEARCH METHOD

Preserve the input.
Question the output.

Detection, tracking and evaluation stay separate. Each stage carries frame indexes, timestamps, configuration and source hashes.

  1. 01

    Normalize & bind

    Preserve originals, normalize once, record source and output hashes.

  2. 02

    Detect & replay

    Resolve Person from model metadata. Replay both trackers on identical detections.

  3. 03

    Review & freeze

    Correct manual boxes and identities. Record uncertainty and explicit human review.

  4. 04

    Evaluate & investigate

    Score frozen intervals and trace misses, duplicates and identity failures to frames.

Current boundary

Human reference QA and an independent test scene are pending. PPE association, safety events, the API and the production dashboard are future phases. This site inspects saved results; it does not run inference or accept uploads.
